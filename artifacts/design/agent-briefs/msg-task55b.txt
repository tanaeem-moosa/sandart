Add the elliptic propagation solve and resolution-parametric levelling diagnostics

Second checkpoint for task #55. Both new gates are OFF by default and the suite
is bit-identical with them off, so nothing shipped changes. Committed to protect
the work and the measuring instruments.

## The finding that reframes task #55

`ticks_to_halve` scales almost exactly LINEARLY with grid width: 522 ticks at
w=512, 65 when normalised by w/64. Linear scaling in domain width is the
signature of a CELL-RATE-LIMITED process — levelling speed is bounded by how
fast information crosses the domain at one cell per tick, not by the magnitude
of the driving potential.

So propagation is the binding constraint and the potential is not. This is why
the free-surface form (`multiplicative_lateral_gate`, previous commit) was a null
at production resolution:

    arch collapse, adaptive:  522 -> 548 ticks  (+5.0% slower)
    arch collapse, perfect:   517 -> 541 ticks  (+4.6% slower)
    pocket equalisation:       29 -> 32 ticks   (~10% slower)
    draining lake peak spread: 159.0 -> 159.0   (bit-identical)

Its earlier 17-24% void-count win at w=512 was isolated to that proxy and does
not appear in any direct levelling measurement.

## The elliptic solve

`elliptic_liquid_level_pass`, gated by `elliptic_head_gate`. Runs once per tick
before the phase loop, reads the tick's frozen `heightmap.data`, and mutates
`temp_heights` directly so the ordinary solver starts from an already-partially
levelled state. Gauss-Seidel rather than Jacobi, every edge step individually
clamped by `clamp_edge_feasible`, sweep direction alternating each iteration.
Gauss-Seidel is sound here — unlike in the wave solver — because this pass
carries no momentum term; it is pure relaxation toward a diffusion equilibrium.

`eta[i] = h_ref[i] + column_depth[i] - row(i) * depth_scale`. The row subtraction
is what makes heads comparable ACROSS rows rather than only within one row, which
is what lets the solve connect two wells through a basin at a different row than
either well. Proven by `test_elliptic_eta_is_row_independent`.

LIQUID-ONLY, by domain restriction rather than by a yield criterion: nodes are
interior in-mask cells with `liquidity >= 0.999`. This is what protects granular
material — `mult_lateral_conveyance` has no yield criterion, so run over sand it
would flatten every settled pile. Verified rather than assumed: with the gate
forced ON, `test_dry_sand_has_angle_of_repose` still passes, 19.29 deg -> 5.07
deg with the non-vacuity anchor at DrySand 3.73 deg against Water 0.00 deg.

## It does NOT remove the propagation limit

| w   | mode        | gate | ticks_to_halve | normalised |
|-----|-------------|------|---------------:|-----------:|
| 128 | adaptive    | OFF  |            128 |       64.0 |
| 128 | adaptive    | ON   |            115 |       57.5 |
| 128 | perfect_sim | OFF  |            136 |       68.0 |
| 128 | perfect_sim | ON   |            112 |       56.0 |
| 512 | adaptive    | OFF  |            522 |      65.25 |
| 512 | adaptive    | ON   |            466 |      58.25 |

The normalised value drops from ~64-68 to ~56-58 at both widths — real and
reproducible — but does not collapse toward a small constant as w grows, which
is what genuine one-tick propagation would look like. Raw ticks still roughly
quadruple from w=128 to w=512, exactly as with the gate off.

Mechanically: `ELLIPTIC_ITERATIONS = 48` is a fixed per-tick budget independent
of w, and a damped Gauss-Seidel sweep reaches on the order of one cell per
iteration. So the pass extends one-cell-per-tick reach to a few-dozen-cells-per-
tick reach: a better constant, not an asymptotic fix. At w=512 the arch gap is
~320 cells and 48 damped iterations cannot span it. Genuine width-independence
needs a multigrid V-cycle (O(log N)) or iterations scaled to the component
diameter.

At small hand-built scale the same mechanism looks like a real fix — arch 71 ->
44 ticks, pockets 45 -> 14 — which is consistent with the diagnosis: fixed reach
covers a small domain and is swamped by a large one.

## Cost: NOT MEASURED, and that is itself a finding

`bench_sandfall` with the gate forced on ran past 12 minutes of wall clock at 96%
CPU without finishing, against a sub-one-minute expectation. No ms/tick number is
recorded here rather than a fabricated one. The pass runs unconditionally over
the whole grid every tick with a 48-iteration inner sweep regardless of whether
any liquid body is out of equilibrium; scoping it to actual disequilibrium, the
way `pressure_project` scopes to touched edges, is a prerequisite to shipping.

## Two bugs found and fixed, worth recording

1. `mult_lateral_conveyance`'s output is unbounded (~h_ref^1.5) and was designed
   to feed `flux_edge_candidate`'s own momentum pipeline, not to be a bare
   multiplier on a direct mass transfer. Used raw, the residual went 0.24 -> 10.9
   -> stuck at 12.8. Fixed with a saturating `weight_e = conv_e / (conv_e +
   ELLIPTIC_CONV_REF)` so a per-edge step can never exceed a damped step toward
   equalising that edge. `test_elliptic_residual_falls_monotonically` guards it.
2. A pass that mutates `temp_heights` is silently reverted next tick unless it
   wakes the blocks it touched: the tick-end copy-back only writes blocks already
   marked `modified`. Symptom was `ticks_to_halve=None` under the adaptive
   scheduler against 12 ticks under perfect simulation on the identical scenario.
   Fixed by walking the pass's own `net_activity` and calling `activate_neighbor`
   on each touched block and its four neighbours. Afterwards adaptive and perfect
   track each other closely, which is independent confirmation that what remains
   is physics rather than scheduling.

## Resolution-parametric diagnostics

The three levelling diagnostics now scale every coordinate from one isotropic
factor and run at w=64/128/256/512 with identical tick budgets per width. Three
traps handled explicitly:

- The arch metric's "3 rows above the pile top" is a fixed CELL count and so a
  different physical height at each width; it now scales with the grid.
- `ProceduralFunnel`'s noise is fixed-PIXEL-frequency, so the clean two-well
  topology exists only at w=128 and fragments into 3-9 pockets at w=256/512.
  `find_two_well_topology` now locates two qualifying wells sharing a basin from
  the mask at each width, verified rather than assumed.
- `ticks_to_halve` counts ticks and scales with w, so a normalised column is
  reported alongside the raw one.
