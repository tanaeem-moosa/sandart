Be concise. Give the conclusion and the one or two things that change what happens
next — not the full reasoning chain, not every caveat, not a survey of options.

**Why:** asked directly on 2026-08-04 after several long design replies: "please be
concise in your communications. if needed I will ask clarification." They are a
capable engineer who can tell when they want more, and long answers cost them
tokens they are actively rationing.

**How to apply:** put the detail in the task ticket, not the reply — tickets are
where this project's context lives anyway. Lead with the answer. Drop the
recap of what they just said. State a caveat once, in a clause, not a section.
Do not pre-empt questions they have not asked. Related: [[delegate-to-sonnet-by-default]].
